FAQ

Questions chicken keepers ask

Is Happy Chicken a complete feed?

No. It is a nutritional supplement and treat. Give it alongside an appropriate complete feed and fresh water.

How much should I give?

A practical guide for adult hens is 10–20 g per bird per day. Introduce gradually and adjust for flock size, age and the rest of the diet.

Do eggs taste different?

Happy Chicken is a supplement, not a flavoring. We do not claim it changes egg taste; results depend on the bird’s complete diet and management.

Is it safe?

It is intended as a supplementary feed for poultry. Follow the feeding guide, keep the product dry and sealed, and consult a veterinarian for birds with specific health needs.

How long does a bag last?

At 10 g per hen per day, 1 kg provides 100 hen-days: about 20 days for 5 hens, 10 days for 10 hens, or 5 days for 20 hens.

Is it suitable for ducks, quail and pheasants?

It can be offered as a supplementary insect treat to other insect-eating poultry and game birds. Use smaller portions for smaller birds, introduce gradually and keep complete feed as the dietary base.
